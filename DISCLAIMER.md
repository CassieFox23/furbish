# Disclaimer

furbish gives a check, not a promise. A clean check doesn't mean a post is good, correct or welcome. It only means the rules furbish knows about didn't catch anything.

Each project has its own rules on AI-assisted posts. Check them before you post, starting with the [open-source AI contribution policies list](https://github.com/melissawm/open-source-ai-contribution-policies).

`--write` changes your draft in place and keeps no backup. Keep your own copy of a draft that matters, or look at the diff first, which is what you get without `--write`.

furbish is GPL-3.0 and comes with no warranty. You decide what to post, and you own what you post.
